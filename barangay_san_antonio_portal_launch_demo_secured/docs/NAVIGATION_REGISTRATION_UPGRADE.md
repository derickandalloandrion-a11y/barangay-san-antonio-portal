# Navigation and Registration

The public navigation and original visual system are retained. Added public destinations include Transparency & Open Data, Citizen’s Charter, Laws & Issuances, FOI, Appointments, Feedback & Complaints, and Privacy Notice.

Resident and Visitor registration is public. Official and Administrator accounts are not created through public registration. Email verification tokens are stored as hashes and consumed once. Local development uses a protected file-preview outbox transport; production SMTP requires configuration and external verification.
